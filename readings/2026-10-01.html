Symbolic Notes
A — The Flattened Receiver
A mailbox is a fixed address made physical, the one point where the outside world is permitted to deposit something into a private life. To crush it in the 9600 block is to sever a numbered threshold mid-digit, leaving the block's final zero with nothing left to receive.
B — The Welfare Ping
A check-welfare call is a system reaching into silence to confirm a signal still exists on the other end. Logged at the day's last hour, it is civic infrastructure performing the oldest ritual: knocking to see if something inside is still alive.
C — The Dormant Load
Listeria's incubation window stretches for weeks, meaning the product can travel, be eaten, and be forgotten before the body ever reports the intrusion. It is contamination structured like a long-delayed letter, addressed to a future date.

Each exhibit is a story about a signal that arrives late, blocked, or unacknowledged — a mailbox destroyed before delivery, a welfare check performed after silence has already set in, a pathogen whose proof of passage takes sixty days to surface.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no record stands alone if it shares a reporting window with another: the sixty-day shelf life opened by City Foods on July 16 runs almost exactly to the week these three logs were filed, meaning the recall's clock, the welfare check's silence, and the mailbox's destruction all close inside the same unacknowledged interval. The Clause does not require causation. It requires only that the custody of the date be shared.
