Symbolic Notes
A — The Barred Threshold
A body raised its voice against a hospital wall for nearly three hours and was told the door would not open again until morning. This is the oldest shape of exile: not banishment, but delay.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, any three incident reports logged within the same twenty-four-hour cycle are considered custodially linked, regardless of jurisdiction, because the clause holds that the day itself — not the desk, not the county — is the true filing cabinet. A hospital's threshold, a toy manufacturer's compress limit, and a Midwestern street corner do not know one another, but the clause does not require that they do. It requires only that they were open at the same time.
