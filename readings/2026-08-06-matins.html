Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, any three public records that surface inside the same seventy-two-hour filing window are presumed causally entangled until the archive proves otherwise, and the archive rarely tries. A cemetery fence does not fail on its own initiative; it is given permission by something upstream, usually a restraint mechanism elsewhere in the custody chain relaxing its grip at the exact hour no one is cross-referencing hours. The Amp Fit arm and the Seymour fence share nothing on paper — no shared county, no shared agency — but the Adjacency Clause does not require paperwork. It requires proximity in the feed. Both records surfaced holding the same idea: a thing meant to hold something in place stopped holding it.

The Cascade
