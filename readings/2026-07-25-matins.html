Symbolic Notes
A — The Porch
The porch is the last threshold before a household's trust becomes visible to strangers; when a package is lifted from it unseen, the theft is really of the assumption that the doorstep is watched.
B — The Snap
A snap is the smallest promise a garment makes: that it will stay closed. When fifty-two of them fail quietly, in ones and twos, it is the failure of a covenant too minor to notice until it is already broken.
C — The License
A suspended license is permission already revoked and still acted upon; the traffic stop simply makes visible a movement that was never sanctioned to begin with.

Porch, snap, and license are the same object wearing three disguises: a small clasp of permission, each one already give-way before anyone thought to check it.
