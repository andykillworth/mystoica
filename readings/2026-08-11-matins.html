Symbolic Notes
A — The Airplane Crash

B — The Boulevard Fire

Flight, fire, and flesh each promise containment — of motion, of structure, of decay — and each promise is the exact place where the record shows failure.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no incident is ever truly singular; it is a node borrowed from a lattice of simultaneous failures elsewhere on the map. A crash filed at an airplane-themed storefront in Indiana does not merely share a news cycle with a fire on a boulevard named for a shipping fortune in Honolulu — it shares a structural grammar: both are transportation metaphors made literal, made physical, made into paperwork. The Chicago meat recall completes the triangle by supplying the third element transportation always requires: cargo. Something was moving, something stopped moving badly, and something inside the cargo was already wrong before the stopping began.

5

Washington, D.C. — By the following week the FSIS alert is finally filed, closing the loop: the crash exposed the fragility of the vehicle, the fire exposed the fragility of the structure, and the recall exposes the fragility of what both were built to carry.

The lattice does not explain itself twice; it only ever shows its hinge once, and today it showed it three times.
