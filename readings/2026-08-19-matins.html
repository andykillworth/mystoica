Symbolic Notes
A — The Scan
The plate inquiry is a confessional performed without the driver's knowledge; the Mercedes carries its owner's unpaid account the way a body carries an old debt, silent until the beam of the scanner touches it.

Scan, jar, and needle are three technologies of puncture — each one built to let something pass from concealment into visibility, and each one caught, on the same forty-eight hours, in the act of doing exactly that.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The standing Adjacency Clause holds that no custody record exists in true isolation — that any two files opened within the same rolling twenty-four-hour window inherit a provisional kinship, a shared file drawer, regardless of jurisdiction. Applied here, the clause binds a license plate in Hudson County, a jar of goat pickle in Travis County, and a syringe in Lawrence County into a single custodial lineage: three seals broken in sequence, each breaking making the next one legible.
