Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that any three public records filed within the same rolling news cycle are never merely coincidental neighbors — they are custodial transfers, each document passing an unclaimed object of harm to the next jurisdiction down the timeline. The fence cannot keep what it lost; the vigil cannot return what it never held; the recall cannot un-recall the fire already lit. What moves between them is not evidence but obligation, and the digit that repeats across unrelated filings is the receipt.
