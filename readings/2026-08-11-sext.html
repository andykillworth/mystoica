Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that no incident is isolated from the incidents filed nearest it in time — that a police blotter, a court docket, and a federal recall notice logged in the same seventy-two-hour window are not coincidental neighbors but co-signers of a single event distributed across jurisdictions. Under this reading, the Segway vendor on Hemlock Road, the dormant theft in the 600 block of Emory Drive, and the jalapeño moving unlabeled through twenty-seven states are not three stories. They are one story, filed in triplicate, each copy redacted by the limits of its own recordkeeping system.
