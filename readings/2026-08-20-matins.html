Symbolic Notes
A — The Severed Cable
A support cable exists to hold tension between two fixed points; when the wire is struck and the rider flung clear, the failure is not of the machine but of the thing that was supposed to catch it.
B — The Unwatched Crossing
A body meets a vehicle at a crosswalk that no agency was standing in; the injury is logged only after the fact, the way all thresholds are guarded in retrospect rather than in advance.
C — The Uninspected Border
Meat crosses a national line and enters commerce before the checkpoint clears it, a shipment moving on the assumption of safety rather than the proof of it.

Each exhibit is a boundary that was trusted to hold and did not — a cable, a curb, a customs stamp — and in each case the failure surfaces only downstream, in a hospital or a warehouse, never at the point of crossing itself.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that no record is orphaned — that any two filings within a shared calendar week are in a custody relationship whether or not the filing agencies ever compare notes. Aventura's beef, Frankfort's pedestrian, and Owensboro's rider never touched, yet each is a variation on the same failed threshold: the reinspection line, the crosswalk line, the tension line. When three unrelated bureaucracies produce the same shaped absence in the same seventy-two hours, the absence itself becomes the document.
