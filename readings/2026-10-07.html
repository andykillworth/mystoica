Symbolic Notes
A — The Unexploded Thing
A device is found not by an officer hunting for danger but by someone checking in on a person they cared for — violence discovered through tenderness, and deferred rather than detonated.
B — The Duplicate Charge
The same offense, filed on the same date, against two residents of the same small town — proof that a single frequency can pass through more than one body and still register as identical.
C — The Guardian Left Behind
The resident escapes; the structure is only partly consumed; but the animal closest to the fire does not leave with her. Exit always has a cost, and the cost is never split evenly.

Read together, the three exhibits trace a single arc: a danger is found before it can go off, the signal duplicates itself to confirm it was real, and finally something pays the price that the rest of the sequence was spared.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no incident is required to share a timeline with the ones it resonates against — only a proximity of form. A thing found before it can go off, a behavior that copies itself exactly, and a death that occurs just out of frame of the human rescue: these are not three unrelated Tuesdays. They are three positions in the same custody chain, each municipality holding the object for exactly as long as it takes to pass it to the next jurisdiction without ever knowing it was handed anything at all.

The Cascade

The chain of custody, reconstructed strictly from the filings themselves:

1

Sweet Home, Oregon — At 10:24 p.m., the device is found inside an act of care — someone checking on a client — and is logged, not as a crime in progress, but as a possession, a thing that already existed before anyone arrived.

2

The item passes out of civilian custody and into the hands of the bomb squad, which does not disarm a threat so much as confirm one: the object is real, and now it is someone else's to hold.

3

North Branford, Connecticut — Three time zones east, the same day's charge appears twice, filed against two separate residents under the identical statute — the signal, unable to detonate in Oregon, resurfaces instead as behavior, splitting itself to prove it was never a single event to begin with.

4

A clause of this kind cannot close on duplication alone. Something in the sequence must convert back from behavior into loss before the custody chain is permitted to end.

5

Dexter, Maine — In the 249 Line Road fire, the human occupant exits the structure; the dog does not. The object that began as an unexploded thing in Oregon and doubled itself in Connecticut completes its transit in Maine as a single, irreversible cost.

The device never went off. It simply kept moving until something agreed to be the ending.
