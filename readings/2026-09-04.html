Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that any three public records filed within the same seventy-two-hour window are not independent — that the calendar itself functions as custodian, binding unrelated filings into a single test of whatever structural question is currently under national strain. This week the strain is legible: every exhibit is a boundary, and every boundary is being asked, under separate jurisdiction, whether it still does its one job.
