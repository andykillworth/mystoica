Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no threshold crossing is isolated — an unattended alarm, an unlogged traveler, and an uninspected shipment are not three separate failures of oversight but one continuous failure occurring in three postal codes at once. The clause holds that inspection is not a constant state but a intermittent visitation, and that the gaps between visitations are where the actual record of the world gets written. What the blotter, the log, and the recall notice share is not content but absence: each is a document of the moment just after something slipped through.
