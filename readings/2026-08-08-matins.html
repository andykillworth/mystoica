Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no filing is an island; any two records entered into the public record within the same reporting cycle inherit a provable custody relationship, regardless of the miles between the county line and the CPSC docket. Proximity in the feed is itself a form of contact.
