Symbolic Notes
A — The Bite on 3 Crabs Road
A bite is the body remembering it has teeth, and 3 Crabs Road is a place named for a creature that walks sideways to survive. The wound falls between two frauds, as if honesty itself needed a mouth to prove it.
B — The Locked Brake
One Honda's decision becomes twenty machines' consequence. The brake is the single true cause hidden inside a crowd of apparent ones — the domino insists it was only following.
C — The First Call at Jacob's Way
Jacob wrestled through the night and would not let go until he was named; the log at his Way opens the same way, at the hour when the day has no name yet, only a number: 12:18.

A bite, a brake, a first call — three small ruptures at the exact seam where one body's motion becomes another body's emergency, proof that the border between self and world is never sealed, only patrolled.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that no incident is filed in true isolation — that geography launders coincidence into proximity, and proximity, held long enough, hardens into cause. A bite on a road named for a sideways-walking animal, a brake locked by a driver who never learned his name made the record, a fire log's first call at an hour still too young to belong to the day: these are not three towns' unrelated Tuesdays. They are one instrument, played across three keys, testing whether the same note struck at 3:30 a.m. in Clallam County and 12:18 a.m. in Bristol County can still be heard as a single sustained tone by the time it reaches print.
