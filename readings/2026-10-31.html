Symbolic Notes
A — The Street Named After the Second Man
Tonti Street carries the name of the companion who stayed behind while the explorer went ahead and vanished. A fire breaking out there at dawn is the record insisting that the second man's name still catches fire before the first man's does.
B — The Cell That Reaches Backward
A man already sealed away by the state uses the one freedom confinement cannot fully remove — the freedom to plan — in order to erase the very event that sealed him. Custody becomes the launching point for its own undoing.
C — The Sliver Inside the Soft Thing
A fragment of hard, clear plastic rides inside a sausage link for three months before anyone bites down on it. The packaging date and the discovery date are never the same date; harm always arrives late to its own scene.

Each exhibit is a sealed container — a structure, a case file, a cardboard box — that held something foreign long before anyone could see it, and all three seals broke in the same week.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The standing Adjacency Clause holds that any two or more hyperlocal public records filed within the same seventy-two-hour window share an unlisted author, and that the records are never about what they claim to be about — a fire, a warrant, a recall — but are fragments of a single instruction being delivered in installments. Today's filing satisfies the Clause three times over: a fire that names its own witness, a prisoner who schemes against his own origin, and a food product that carries its injury inside itself for months before confessing. The Clause does not require intent. It requires only that the documents be real, dated, and filed by someone paid to notice small things.

The Cascade

A five-step custody trace, filed in the order the record permits, not the order the events occurred.

1

La Salle, Illinois — At 9:15 a.m., Tonti Street ignites — a street named for the man who was left behind, now the first address to catch fire in this chain.

2

Four departments converge on one block, which the project reads as four outside jurisdictions agreeing, without discussion, that a single site deserves full attention.

3

Stafford County, Virginia — Inside Rappahannock Regional Jail, a man already in custody for one offense spends months quietly building a second one, aimed backward at the person who put him there.

4

McCook, Illinois — Three months earlier, on June 23, a sausage link is packed with a sliver of hard plastic already riding inside it, sealed into a box and shipped out before anyone knows it is armed.

5

In the last week of October, the fire, the plot, and the plastic all surface in public record within days of one another, as if concealment itself has a shelf life and this was simply the week it expired.

The dossier does not allege causation. It notes only that three sealed containers failed in the same window, and that each failure was reported by someone whose job is to notice such things.
