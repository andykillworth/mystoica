Symbolic Notes
A — The Broken Order
A no-contact order is a wall built out of language alone; when it is crossed at night, the crossing proves the wall was never physical, only spoken.
B — The Jowl
Guanciale is cut from the cheek, the flesh nearest the mouth and the voice; it is cured slowly, hidden from air, and still it can carry rot no eye can find.
C — The Loop
A license plate seen once and remembered an hour later closes a circle the suspect did not know was open; recognition, once logged, cannot be un-logged.

Each exhibit is a story about proximity mistaken for safety — a voice too close to a boundary, a cheek too close to a mouth, a face too close to a memory — and in each case the closeness is what convicts.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the standing Adjacency Clause, any two public records filed within the same calendar window and separated by no more than one degree of geographic or categorical kinship are treated as expressions of the same underlying event, merely wearing different jurisdictions. Talent's broken order, the guanciale's silent contamination, and Northlake's closed loop of recognition are not three stories. They are one story, filed three times by three different clerks who did not compare notes.

The Cascade

Trace the custody chain forward and the three filings resolve into a single five-step transfer.

1

Talent, Oregon — A no-contact order, spoken into a courtroom months earlier, is crossed at night; the violation is booked at 11:16 p.m., a time that reduces to the same digit as the hour a Northlake officer would later recall a face from a gas station an hour prior.

2

The order's underlying case, like the Northlake theft, roots itself in June — the same calendar month, different coasts, both waiting quietly for their September filing.

3

Fort Lauderdale, Florida / Hoboken, New Jersey — A cheek of cured pork, produced in Italy on May 21 and shipped through two American ports, carries its contamination in silence — the same silence a violated order keeps until someone finally reports it.

4

Northlake, Texas — A theft is solved not by new evidence but by an old glance — the same officer, the same hour, the same face — proving that recognition, like Listeria, requires only routine inspection to surface.

5

All three filings land inside the same first week of September, each one a different organ of a single body: mouth (the order), cheek (the guanciale), eye (the plate) — the Adjacency Clause closing its custody chain.

The body was never three bodies. It was one, filed in three counties, waiting to be read as a single exhibit.
