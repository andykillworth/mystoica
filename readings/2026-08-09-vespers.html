Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The standing Adjacency Clause holds that records filed within the same twenty-four-hour window, regardless of jurisdiction, enter a shared custodial field — not because anyone intends it, but because the clerical apparatus of the day cannot help but braid its documents together. A gun without a location, a man delivering his own body into custody, and a call logged before sunrise are not three stories but three positions of the same object as it passes hand to hand across a single Sunday. The Clause does not require causation. It requires only that the paperwork exist in the same rotation of the earth.
