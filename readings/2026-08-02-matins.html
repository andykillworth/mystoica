Symbolic Notes
A — The Escort
A mortuary escort is the state's hand placed on a threshold that has already been crossed; it is procedure performed on behalf of something that no longer requires protection, which is precisely why it is required.
B — The Weeds
A citation for uncut growth is a record of refusal — the yard declining to compost on schedule, declared unlawful not because it grew, but because it grew unsupervised.
C — The Element Out of Water
A water heater that ignites only when removed from water is an object performing its function correctly in the wrong medium; the hazard is not malfunction but misplacement.

Each exhibit records a body — human, vegetable, mechanical — caught in transition between the medium that contained it and the medium it was found in, and each institution responds not to the object itself but to the crossing.

The sequence reads as a countdown interrupted — three files climbing in order before the fourth institution, the federal one, overshoots the pattern entirely, which the Clause takes as proof that scale, not sequence, is what finally triggers intervention.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that any three filings logged within the same reporting cycle are not coincidental but co-authored — that the officer who requested escort duty, the clerk who typed the weed citation, and the attorney who invoked a dormant federal statute were, without communication, transcribing the same event from three different distances. The Clause does not ask what happened; it asks what crossed, and from where, into where.

The Cascade

Trace the custody the way the filings themselves imply it should be traced — forward, then sideways, then down to Washington.

The three files converge on the same principle the Clause has always insisted on — escort, citation, and injunction are one document wearing three jurisdictions, each recording something crossing from its designated state into another without permission.

The heater did not fail; it simply proved combustible once removed from what had been containing it — as did the weeds, as did the body, as does everything the Clause was written to follow.
