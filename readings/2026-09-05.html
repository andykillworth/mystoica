Symbolic Notes
A — The Borrowed Face
A false ID is a small forged self, a face borrowed to cross a threshold it was not built for. Its destruction at the point of discovery does not erase the crossing it already made.
B — The Altered Seal
A tampered public record is the borrowed face grown up: no longer a costume for one night but an intervention in the permanent archive, a lie built to outlive its author.
C — The Hidden Pepper
An undeclared ingredient traveling downstream through establishments that never asked its origin is the same deception at civilizational scale — a false identity smuggled inside the food chain itself, multiplying with every relabeling.

Each exhibit is a different scale of the same offense: a credential, a record, and a foodstuff all pretending to be something safer than they are, and all discovered only by accident of routine inspection.
