Three perimeters were tested this week, and three systems built to notice a trespass noticed one — a wire in the lake country, a label crossing an ocean, and a single added letter crossing a desk in Alexandria.

Symbolic Notes
A — The Alarm
A triggered wire that finds no intruder is not a false alarm; it is a boundary announcing that it was tested and held, three times, in the same six days.
B — The Biscuit
A product named Evergreen conceals, inside its own claim to permanence, the two things it swore it did not contain — the perennial undone by the undeclared.
C — The Word
One letter inserted into a claimed name is still a trespass; the Board does not measure intent, only proximity, and proximity was enough.

Wire, label, and letter are the same sentinel wearing three disguises — each exists only to detect the moment something gets close enough to a claimed boundary to be mistaken for what it is not.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no three public records surfacing in the same reporting window are coincidental; they are instances of a single boundary-maintenance signal, refracted through whichever bureaucracy happened to be listening. This week the signal traveled through a rural alarm panel, a federal food-safety feed, and a quasi-judicial trademark docket, each one logging the identical event: something came close enough to a protected line to be flagged, and was stopped before it could be mistaken for the real thing.

The Cascade

Custody trace, perimeter to label to register:

1

Crosslake, Minnesota — The wire on Birch Narrows Road trips at 10:20 p.m.; no intruder is found, but the boundary is now awake and logged.

2

The same waking signal, uncredited, rides forward inside a shipment of biscuit tins already crossing international waters.

3

Shanghai, China — The tin's label omits two of its true contents — shrimp, milk — until the federal alert updates on Nov. 20–21 to say so.

4

The omission, now named, migrates out of the pantry and into language itself, surfacing as a dispute over one added letter.

5

Alexandria, Virginia — The Board rules the added letter cannot stand; the boundary, breached three times in three vocabularies this week, closes in the same week it opened.

What begins as a tripped wire in the lake country ends as a denied syllable in Alexandria; the perimeter, wherever it is drawn, holds.
