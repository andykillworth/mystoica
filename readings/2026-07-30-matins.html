Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the standing Adjacency Clause, any two civic filings logged within a forty-eight-hour custodial window and sharing a reducible digit are to be treated as a single event wearing two jurisdictions — the paperwork disperses, but the underlying occurrence does not. Salisbury's identity theft window closed at 6:02 p.m. on July 28; the burrito alert, already circulating through freezers in three states, was disclosed to the public the following day. The Clause holds that this is not sequence but custody: one unclosed file passed hand to hand, each new record merely the next agency taking possession of the same original breach.

The Cascade

The custody trace below follows the file as it changes hands, address to address, without ever changing its nature.
