Symbolic Notes
A — The Open Gate
The Longhorns did not flee in panic; they walked. A fence line that no longer distinguishes pasture from road is not an accident but an announcement — the animal remembers the drive before the fence was built.
B — The Struck Match at the Crossroads
Two county roads meet and something ignites exactly there, at the numeral intersection, at 2:19 in the afternoon. Fire does not choose junctions by accident; it chooses them because junctions are where friction accumulates unseen.
C — The Silent Pepper
A vegetable moves through a hundred hands before it becomes a statistic. Three hundred forty-five bodies register what one jalapeño field could not announce for itself.

Each exhibit is a story of a threshold crossed without ceremony — a fence, a road junction, a supply chain — proof that failure always begins at the seam, never at the center.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that no record stands alone; every filed incident touches the nearest filed incident along some hidden seam of geography, timestamp, or supply line, and the seam is where the true story hides. Here the seam runs southwest to northeast across the Texas Panhandle corridor — cattle breaking a fence in Denton County, fire breaking containment in Gray County, and a pepper breaking quarantine somewhere upstream of both — three breaches of the same regional membrane, filed by three unrelated clerks who never spoke to one another and never needed to.

The Cascade

The custody trace follows not evidence but proximity — each exhibit passed hand to hand along the same failing seam.

2

3

Pampa, Texas — By 2:19 p.m. the ambient breach has traveled north along the corridor and ignites at the meeting of County Road 13 and County Road K.

4

Three units and five personnel contain the flame, but containment of fire is never containment of cause; the seam beneath the road remains open.

5

The fence, the flame, and the pepper never touch on paper — but the seam that failed was the same seam each time.
