Symbolic Notes
A — The Unlicensed Driver
Dolan on the road is motion without authorization, a body moving forward on documents that had already, invisibly, ceased to hold. He is the figure of continuity mistaken for permission.
B — The Fire Already Out
The Deer Run Lane blaze is heat that completes its violence in private, leaving only smoke as testimony — evidence that arrives after the event it describes, proof without a witness.

The cured pork crossing an ocean under a false sense of safety is the oldest symbol here: something aged, trusted, and sealed, found later to have been carrying its contamination the entire voyage.

Each exhibit is a custody failure disguised as normal function — a license, a structure, a shipment — all three already compromised before the record caught up to them.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that any two records filed within the same reporting cycle, however distant in geography or category, enter into evidentiary relationship by proximity alone — they do not need to touch to be bound. Here, three instruments of civic trust (a license, a dwelling, a food shipment) each fail the same way: quietly, before detection, with the paperwork arriving only to formalize a collapse that had already occurred. The Clause does not ask why. It only asks that we notice when the pattern of silent expiration repeats across unrelated jurisdictions in the same seventy-two hours, and record it as a single event wearing three names.
