Symbolic Notes
A — The Leaf Blower
An engine built to move air becomes, in the wrong hands, a small theft of wind itself — the tool for clearing debris repurposed as the debris-maker.
B — The Jalapeño
Heat that was meant to stay contained inside a single pepper skin instead travels the length of a continent, disguised inside twenty-two states' worth of ordinary dinners.
C — The Gas Leak
An odorless current escapes a pipe at dusk on Fir Street, the most patient of the three vapors, waiting only for a spark it never gets.

Wind, heat, and vapor — three invisible mediums, each stolen or leaked from its intended vessel on the same seven-day arc, each capable of traveling farther than the hand that released it.

Where the eight repeats, the record shows a threshold being crossed rather than closed: 8 is the number of a door left ajar, and both currents — the stolen wind, the hidden heat — pass through it at nearly the same civic hour.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under what this project has come to call the Adjacency Clause, no public record exists in true isolation; each filing carries a residue of the others logged within the same reporting week, however distant their jurisdictions. The clause does not require that Manassas, Washington, and Pampa share a sender — only that they share a Thursday, and that a Thursday is a wide enough net to catch three currents mid-flight and force them into the same drawer.

The Cascade

The custody trace follows not an object but a medium, passed hand to hand across three unrelated case files.
