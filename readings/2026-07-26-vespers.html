Symbolic Notes
A — The Cellphone-Blade
The argument begins as signal — voice, text, the small electric grievance of a phone — before it is translated into steel. The wound is the message arriving in a body instead of a screen.
B — The Red Flag Line
Fire does not attack; it inherits. The mountain simply accepts what the dry air and the gusting wind hand it, and a control line is drawn to say: past this point, custody ends.
C — The Loosened Pearl
Ornament detaches from function. The pearl was sewn on to be looked at, not held by, and it comes away exactly where a small mouth is waiting.

Each exhibit records something small breaking its bond with something larger — a conversation from civility, a hillside from its own stillness, a bead from its sandal — and in each case the break is what gets filed.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no record stands alone if it was filed within the same rolling weekend as another; proximity in the docket is treated as evidence of shared cause, regardless of geography. A blade drawn in Jamestown on a Saturday afternoon does not merely coincide with a red flag warning issued the same day in Prineville — it is read as the same pressure surfacing twice, once in tissue, once in timber. The pearl that comes loose in a Cincinnati news report a day later is not an aftershock so much as the clause's third and final witness: proof that whatever separated a body from civility and a hillside from its calm was still traveling when it reached the smallest fastening in the chain, a snap sewn onto a child's sandal.

The Cascade

The custody trace follows one instability as it changes shape across three jurisdictions and five days.

1

Jamestown, New York — A phone call is not answered the way it was meant to be, and the disagreement is carried, still live, into a blade.

2

The wound is logged, the suspect is booked, and the incident closes at the level of the individual — but the pressure that produced it does not close with it.

3

Prineville, Oregon — The same weekend, at continental distance, dry air and wind arrive together over Green Mountain, and Incident 611 is opened under conditions that make control lines necessary rather than optional.

4

Madras, Oregon — The Ten Mile Fire holds its acreage rather than grows it, the line becomes the record of where the instability was finally arrested — contained, not extinguished.

5

Cincinnati, Ohio — What could not be arrested crosses one more boundary, surfacing not as flame or blade but as a decorative pearl no longer willing to stay fixed to a child's shoe, and the recall notice becomes the clause's final signature.

*The instability was never local; it was only ever waiting for its next fastening to fail.*
