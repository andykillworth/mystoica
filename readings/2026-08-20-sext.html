Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that no record is filed in isolation — that a name, once written into one ledger, obligates its neighbors across distance and jurisdiction. Here the clause tightens around a single surname. Crumpton is charged in Jefferson County, Tennessee, at the exact hour that a fire company bearing the same name sits listed in the mutual-aid roster of a Maryland station dispatching to Millington. A name does not repeat by accident. It repeats because the custody chain requires a witness at both ends.
