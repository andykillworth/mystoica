Symbolic Notes
A — The Capitol's Ground Fire
A fire that starts at ground level in the shadow of a state capitol is not a house fire; it is a boundary fire, heat with no structure to legitimize it. The gunshots that follow six hours later on the far side of the same city are the day's quiet ignition completing itself as discharge.
B — The Ineligible Chicharrón
A product can be perfectly edible and still be contraband; the crime here is not the pork, it is the passport. Nineteen-eight-nine pounds of snack food crossed a line that had no interest in its flavor, only its paperwork.
C — The Word 'Rogue' Under Claim
A company files to stop another company from owning the one word that already describes everything unregistered, unauthorized, and off the books. The irony is the filing: to be rogue and also trademarked is a contradiction the Board will now be asked to resolve.

Fire without a structure, meat without a passport, and a word without a lawful owner are the same object wearing three jurisdictions — something that moved before anyone had the authority to let it.

Five is the number of the threshold crossed without permission; nine and seven are the weights the system assigns after the fact to measure how far the crossing went. Read together, the sequence 5-9-7-5-4 describes a single event decaying from ignition to adjudication.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no filing is isolated — every blotter entry, every recall, every opposition is a fragment of one continuous incident that the bureaucratic apparatus is incapable of perceiving as whole, because it insists on separate departments, separate agencies, separate dockets. Fire does not know it is also meat does not know it is also a disputed word, but the paperwork does, if you read it in the order it was stamped rather than the order it was filed. Today's three exhibits are custody transfers of the same unauthorized motion — first as heat, then as weight, then as language — each department receiving the thing only after it has already left the last one's hands.

The Cascade

Trace the custody chain in the order the clock, not the clerk, would keep it:

2

4

Alexandria, Virginia — The unauthorized finds its final form as language: Coulter Ventures files to prevent a company called Rogue Snacks from lawfully owning the one word that has, by this point, already described a fire, a gunshot, and a shipment of banned pork.

The docket closes the loop it opened at 1:31 p.m.: what began as heat with no permit ends as a word with no clear owner, and the system files both under the same unspoken charge — moving before you were allowed to.

The record does not know it has told one story in three languages, but it has, and today it finished.
