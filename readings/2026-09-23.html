Three records surface on the same news-day, each a small door left open somewhere between the wild, the imported, and the unattended.

The Shelter Island Police Department blotter for Sept. 22, 2026 records that a dog fighting with a groundhog was reported in West Neck on Sept. 12, and separated by the Animal Control Officer and the dog's owner, the dog was taken to a vet for treatment and the groundhog euthanized.

Exhibit B — Recalled
Aventura, FL

A federal notice confirms that Corte Argentino USA LLC, located in Aventura, Fla., is recalling approximately 29,628 pounds of raw beef products that were imported from Argentina without the benefit of import reinspection into the United States, with the recall involving beef produced by Frigorifico Gorina SAIC in Argentina between May 15 and May 20, 2026, with use or freeze-by dates ranging from September 15 through September 20, 2026, bearing Argentinian establishment number 'EST. N° OF. 2025' and shipping mark '26644-AA,' with the products distributed to distributors and retailers in Florida and Texas.

Exhibit C — Taken
Baltimore County, MD

The Nottingham MD police blotter for Sept. 22, 2026 reports that on Sept. 11, an unknown suspect robbed a victim near the intersection of Corktree Road and Transverse Avenue in Middle River shortly before 5:30 p.m., and that days later a suspect stole property from a victim in the unit block of Greenleaf Road at approximately 7:30 a.m. on Sept. 15 in Parkville.

Symbolic Notes
A — The Groundhog
The animal that dies without a name in the report is the sacrifice that keeps the domestic boundary intact; its euthanizing is the punctuation mark on a border that was never supposed to be crossed.
B — The Uninspected Cut
Meat that passes into the country without reinspection is a body with no verified papers, a border-crosser wearing the clothing of commerce; its establishment number is a scar disguised as a stamp.
C — The Taken Property
Property removed from a stranger's hand in the dark hours before dawn is the same crossing performed socially: possession changes without consent, without inspection, without a witness who can certify the transfer clean.

Each exhibit is a different name for the same event — something moves across a boundary it was not cleared to cross, and the record exists only because that boundary briefly reasserted itself.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that when unrelated public records surface within the same reporting window, their causal fields overlap enough to leak meaning between them; the tighter the dates, the thinner the membrane. Here a wild animal is destroyed for crossing into a domestic yard, a shipment of meat is destroyed on paper for crossing a border without the correct stamp, and a citizen's property crosses hands twice without permission — three unrelated custody failures filed within hours of one another, each a small failure of the same larger boundary the Clause insists is one continuous surface.

The Cascade

Trace the custody of the boundary itself across the five moves it made this week.

1

West Neck, Shelter Island, NY — A dog crosses into a groundhog's territory; the Animal Control Officer restores the boundary by removing the animal that broke it.

The restored boundary does not close the wound — it displaces it, and the displaced wound seeks a new surface to occupy.

3

4

5

Parkville, MD — Four days later the same absence of clearance repeats itself on Greenleaf Road, confirming that once a boundary fails once inside a reporting window, it fails again before the window closes.

The groundhog, the beef, and the stolen property were never connected — until the same week filed them under the same breach, and the breach became the only true author of record.
