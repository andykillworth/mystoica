Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, filings that surface within the same reporting window are treated as a single distributed event rather than three coincidences — the custodial lapse in New York does not cause the contamination in Illinois or the ceiling in Kentucky, but all three are read as symptoms surfacing along the same fault line of unwatched systems, each filed by a clerk who never spoke to the other two.

The Cascade

Trace the custody chain in five moves, each one passing the failure forward without ever naming it.

1

Batavia, New York — A parent's attention detaches from a child mid-afternoon; the street receives what the house could not hold.

2
