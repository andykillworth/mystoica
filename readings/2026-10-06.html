Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no public record is an island; any three filings that surface within the same seventy-two-hour window and share a structural verb — here, to falsify — are treated as custodial transfers of the same original offense, each jurisdiction merely holding the evidence until the next one claims it.
