Symbolic Notes
A — The Main Street Return
A single artery hosts a crash, a fraud, and a citation within seventeen hours, the street looping back on itself like a wound that will not close.

A product exists entirely outside inspection, proof that provision does not require a watching eye, only a hungry mouth waiting on the other end.

Each exhibit marks a different failure of witnessing — the street watched but not corrected, the label watched but not truthful, the meat never watched at all — three widening circles around the same absent inspector.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that any two records filed within the same reporting window inherit a procedural residue from one another, regardless of distance. A Bluffton intersection, a chicken breast in nine refrigerators, and a cut of meat with no inspector's stamp did not choose each other, but the clause insists proximity in time is a kind of custody. Once filed together, they are legally — symbolically — bound.
