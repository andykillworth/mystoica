Symbolic Notes
A — The Extinguisher Thrown Blind
A tool meant to suppress is hurled over a wall instead of used, discarded before it could ever be called on. It becomes evidence of intent rather than instrument of prevention.
B — The Cheese That Carried It
Raw milk, aged and trusted, becomes a vehicle for something it was never supposed to hold. The contamination travels quietly through distribution lines long before anyone tastes what's wrong.
C — The Structure Fully Engulfed
By the time responders arrive, there is no early stage left to catch — only defensive posture, mutual aid, and the long work of keeping damage from spreading further than it already has.

Each exhibit marks a different point on the same curve: the barrier discarded, the contamination incubating unseen, and the inevitable full engulfment that follows when nothing was left standing between cause and consequence.

Two systems of containment — a shelf life and a two-company fire response — collapse onto the same reduced value, suggesting that spoilage and suppression are, numerically, the same operation running in opposite directions.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that no record exists in true isolation — that a discarded extinguisher in one jurisdiction pre-writes the shortage of extinguishing capacity in another, days later and a thousand miles off. Lewiston's fire extinguisher was never deployed; it was thrown, unused, into a stranger's yard. That unused capacity does not vanish. It migrates. It surfaces next in Worcester, where a dairy's raw product — itself a kind of trust extinguished — carries contamination no barrier caught in time. And it surfaces finally in Fish Bay, where the fire that needed early suppression instead arrived to find the structure already fully engulfed, the moment for a small intervention long past. The clause does not claim causality in the ordinary sense. It claims adjacency: that readiness withheld in one place is readiness missing in another.

The Cascade

The custody trace follows the extinguisher's absence as it moves through three unrelated jurisdictions in eleven days.

1

Lewiston, New York — October 5 — a fire extinguisher is thrown over a backyard fence instead of used, removing one unit of suppression capacity from circulation without explanation.

2

Lewiston, New York — Patrol cannot make contact with the thrower; the extinguisher is logged as found, not returned to service, and the file is closed as a report rather than a resolution.

3

Worcester, New York — In the eleven days that follow, a lot of raw cheddar cheese moves through distribution bearing a hidden E. coli contamination that no inspection barrier intercepts before it reaches shelves.

4

Worcester, New York — The recall is announced October 15 — the contamination event closing out exactly as the extinguisher's absence reaches its tenth day unaccounted for.

5

Fish Bay, St. John — October 15 into 16 — a structure fire is reported already fully engulfed; no early suppression reaches it in time, and sixteen personnel are required where one unit, deployed early, might once have sufficed.

What is thrown away in one town is simply missing, later, in another.
