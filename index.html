Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that when unrelated custodial records — a town's overnight patrol log, a federal food recall, a trademark docket — cluster inside the same narrow calendar window, they are not coincidence but exposure: the same latent anxiety about ownership and purity surfacing simultaneously in three unrelated bureaucratic registers, each one a different dialect for asking the same question — who is allowed to say this belongs to me, and who checks that nothing has changed in the night.
