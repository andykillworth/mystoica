Three unconnected municipal records surfaced within the same forty-eight-hour window, and read together they describe a single body of evidence moving quietly between jurisdictions.

Symbolic Notes
A — The Wanderer
An old man steps out of his address and briefly becomes pure coordinates — an intersection, a timestamp, a description of a coat — before being pulled back into a name. He is memory leaving its container and, for a few hours, succeeding.
B — The Unsecured Document
A passport is the only object designed to prove a person is who they say they are, and on this day one simply walked out of the 00 block of Willow Road unattended, the way a man walks out of a house on Waterfront Drive.
C — The Second Flame
Fire that returns to the same crew on consecutive nights is not an accident of chance but a signature — the basement, then the oven, as if the first fire was only a rehearsal for where it actually needed to go.

A man loses his place, a document loses its owner, and a fire loses patience with waiting a single night — three records of identity refusing to stay fixed to one address.

The digit 4 surfaces twice — once in the street where a man was found, once buried in the case number of a document that was not — and 2 echoes it, marking both the hour of his sighting and the two nights fire needed to finish its work.

Four is the number of the fixed address, the place a thing is supposed to stay; its repetition here, bracketing a vanished man and a vanished passport, reads as the record insisting on return even as its subjects insist on leaving.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no municipal record exists in true isolation — a destabilized identity in one jurisdiction will produce a corresponding paperwork failure or combustion event in another within a 48-hour custody window, the three forming a single displaced file that no single department is equipped to recognize as connected. Here the wanderer, the missing passport, and the twice-struck match are not three stories but one story, filed in three incompatible systems.

The Cascade

The custody trace runs as follows, address to address, record to record:

1

Washington's Landing, Pittsburgh — Early afternoon, October 23: William Getty leaves the address on Waterfront Drive that has, until this moment, verified his identity.

2

Lawrenceville, Pittsburgh — At 7:40 p.m. he is sighted at 40th and Butler — a coordinate, not yet a name again — and then the trail goes administratively cold.

3

Menlo Park, California — In the same rolling window, at 15:01, a passport is reported lost from the 00 block of Willow Road: the documentary twin of Getty's unmoored identity, surfacing three time zones away.

4

Newton, New Jersey — A basement ignites on Walker Street — the first combustion event in the custody chain, logged but not yet understood as part of the sequence.

5

Newton, New Jersey — The following evening the oven ignites on Carriage Lane, closing the loop at the exact hour Getty is found safe in Pittsburgh — two fires required where one would not confirm the pattern.

The man returned home, the passport presumably did not, and the fire, having said what it needed to say twice, did not return a third time.
