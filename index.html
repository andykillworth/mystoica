Mainstream press excluded

Three files were opened on the same rolling weekend, and each one records a surface that did not match what it was supposed to certify.

Exhibit A — Filed
Elgin, Illinois

On October 24, 2026, Imperio Distribution LLC, an Elgin, Illinois establishment, recalled approximately 946 pounds of ready-to-eat beef jerky products that were produced without the benefit of federal inspection and misbranded because of the unauthorized use of a USDA mark of inspection. The recalled packages bore the unapproved mark of another facility, identified as establishment number 1885 inside the USDA seal.

Exhibit B — Filed
Perry, New York

On October 22, 2026, the Perry Police Department arrested 19-year-old Bradley D. Weis of Castile, New York, on a bench warrant after he failed to appear in Perry Village Court on multiple dates, following an original charge of aggravated unlicensed operation. Weis was arraigned in Warsaw Town Court and committed to the Wyoming County Jail with bail set at $2,000 cash, $4,000 bond, and $8,000 partially secured bond.

Exhibit C — Filed
Pamplin, Virginia

Around 5:24 p.m. on Saturday, October 24, 2026, crews from the Pamplin Volunteer Fire Department were called to the 200 block of Dungee Road in Appomattox County, where first-arriving responders found the structure fully engulfed in flames and had it extinguished by 5:54 p.m.

Symbolic Notes
A — The Borrowed Mark
Meat wearing another facility's seal is a lie stamped into flesh before it ever reaches a shelf — identity theft performed on a product instead of a person.
B — The Bench Warrant
A name that will not stay in the room assigned to it, slipping out of scheduled court dates the way smoke slips a flue before anyone smells it.
C — The Engulfed House
For thirty minutes a dwelling stops being a structure with an address and becomes only combustion, a building erased by its own material.

Each exhibit is a record of something failing to remain what its paperwork claimed: the jerky under a stolen seal, the man absent from his assigned docket, the house dissolved past the point of being a house.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The standing Adjacency Clause holds that records filed within the same seventy-two-hour window, regardless of agency or jurisdiction, enter a shared custodial field — and here the clause tightens around a specific failure mode: agricultural identity, judicial identity, and structural identity all broke in the same cycle, each one a different bureaucracy's version of the sentence 'this is not what it says it is.' The clause does not claim causation. It claims proximity is itself a form of evidence.

The Cascade

Trace the custody of the lie through its three containers — product, person, and structure — in the order the paperwork was opened.

1

5

no fixed location — By nightfall, three unrelated agencies — a federal recall desk, a county court clerk, a volunteer fire log — close their files within hours of each other, each one stating the same fact in a different vocabulary: the record caught up to the object too late.

It was never the jerky, the warrant, or the house. It was the lag between what a thing is and what its paperwork insists it still claims to be.
