Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, any three filings that land in the same seven-day custody window are treated not as coincidence but as a single distributed record, artificially separated by jurisdiction and agency letterhead. The Clause holds that a county blotter, a federal recall notice, and a trademark opposition are not different kinds of document at all — they are the same intake report, filed in triplicate by bureaucracies that do not speak to one another, each one logging an unauthorized crossing of a threshold meant to stay closed.
