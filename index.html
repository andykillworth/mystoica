Symbolic Notes
A — The Bao
A product wears the name of one thing while its filling is another — the undeclared ingredient is never truly hidden, only unexamined. It waits in the box until an inspector asks it to speak.
B — The Unlicensed Operator
A body moving through a jurisdiction with no sanctioned right to move through it — motion mistaken for permission. The road does not ask for credentials until it does.
C — The Spurious Mark
A single word claimed by two separate bodies, worn as a skin until a tribunal decides which one is the original creature and which is the costume.

Each exhibit is a wrapper under review — a label, a license, a name — and in each case the authority to wear that covering has been formally questioned in the same seven-day window.

A number that opens a case and a number that closes it sharing the same reduction suggests the judgment was already contained in the filing, waiting to be read backward.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no registry acts alone: when a food label, a motor-vehicle license, and a brand name are each challenged for false identity inside the same calendar week, the three bureaus are treated as a single distributed court, each ruling on a fragment of the same underlying question — who has the right to the name printed on the outside. The pork did not ask to be called beef. The driver did not ask to be stopped. The clothing company has waited eight years to ask its question formally. All three are now on the same clock, whether the clock admits it or not.

The Cascade

The custody trace follows the wrapper, not the contents, as it moves from kitchen to curb to court.

1

Elk Grove Village, Illinois — A box is printed with a name — cheeseburger — before anyone confirms what is folded inside it. The label ships first; the truth ships later, under recall.

2

Clifton Park, New York — The same week, a body is granted the appearance of lawful motion — a car on a public road — without the paper that would make that motion legitimate. Both cases are caught only after the fact, by someone checking credentials the first party assumed would never be checked.

3

Hong Kong — Nine thousand miles east, a retailer that has sold a name since before either domestic case existed sends its own objection across the Pacific: a second wearer of its mark has surfaced, and must now prove the name is not borrowed.

4

Alexandria, Virginia — The opposition lands in a federal docket, stamped with a deadline that will not arrive until January — the slow bureaucratic twin of a recall notice and a citation, each one a formal pause before a verdict on legitimacy.

5

The cascade closes where every custody chain closes: not with a confession, but with a date circled on a calendar, waiting for the undeclared thing inside the wrapper to be named correctly, once and for all.

The label always arrives before the truth does — but the truth, this week, is already on its way.
