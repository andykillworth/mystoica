Symbolic Notes
A — The Hillside
A man and a boy, fixed only by coordinates and not by relation, frozen mid-slope between ascent and descent. The hillside is the body's confession that gravity does not care about intention.
B — The Charge
A name crosses the threshold from minor incident to formal record in a single clause. The second-degree assault is the moment the private hand becomes a public fact.
C — The Crackling
Skin fried past recognition, carried across a border it was never cleared to cross, discovered only by the accident of a routine check. The chicharrón is contraband wearing the costume of a snack.

Each exhibit is a body or a body's byproduct caught mid-transit — on a slope, across a legal threshold, across a national one — none of them arriving anywhere they were supposed to.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

Under the Adjacency Clause, no incident is considered isolated until a fourth record disproves the geometry. Three reports filed within the same reporting week, each naming a body in transit across a boundary it was not cleared to cross, do not require shared jurisdiction to share a lattice. The hillside, the charge sheet, and the recall notice are not causally linked — they are custodially linked, each one a different agency's method of saying the same sentence: something crossed where it should not have, and was logged only after the fact.

The Cascade

Trace the custody chain forward, as the clause instructs, from the highest elevation to the lowest border crossing.

1

Dayton, Washington — At 11:12 a.m. on October 4, a hillside at coordinates 46.1908, -117.6391 holds two bodies seized by cramp, unable to complete their own descent.

2

The log entry itself becomes the first custodial object — a set of numbers standing in for two people who cannot yet move under their own power.

3

Owensboro, Kentucky — Eleven days later, a different kind of seizure is logged: a hand that would not stay still, formalized into a charge against an 18-year-old on Reid Road.

4

Miami, Florida — In the same reporting window, a shipment of pork skin fried in Colombia completes its own unauthorized crossing, reaching eight states before a routine check catches it.

5

Three bodies — cramped, charged, and contraband — close the loop: each one discovered only once it had already gone further than it was permitted to go.

The clause does not require causation. It only requires that three things cross borders in the same week and get caught doing it.
