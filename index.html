Three unconnected municipalities logged three separate responses to heat that had no one home to claim it, and the paperwork, read together, tells its own story.

Symbolic Notes
A — The Therapy K9
An animal sent not to arrest but to absorb, dispatched into a household already burning from the inside with a conflict no one could extinguish by hand.
B — The Woodstove of Unknown Verdict
A heat source installed for comfort, used by no one present to answer for it, left to the fire marshal as a closed file stamped undetermined.
C — The Defensive Perimeter
A house already known to be empty, its fire fought not to save a life inside but simply to keep the flame from spreading past its own forgotten boundary.

Each exhibit is a public agency arriving to manage heat generated by absence — emotional, custodial, or structural — where the responders become the only witnesses the event will ever have.

Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that any two or more infrastructural responses — police, fire, animal control — occurring within an eighteen-hour window across unconnected jurisdictions are presumed to share custody of a single unresolved thermal event, even when no shared cause can be filed. The clause does not require proximity in space, only proximity in the hour a dispatcher's voice went out. Under this reading, Madison's K9, Skowhegan's woodstove, and Urbana's vacant rear wall are not three fires but one fire, passed hand to hand through three separate incident numbers.

The Cascade

The custody trace follows the heat as it changes hands across three departments in under thirty-six hours.

1

Madison, Wisconsin — At 1:18 p.m., a therapy K9 is deployed into a residence where two people already share an address and a disorder neither can extinguish; the heat is domestic, interior, and officially unlogged as anything but conduct.

2

Within hours the heat leaves the body and enters the structure — the clause requires no mechanism for this, only coincidence of hour.

3

Skowhegan, Maine — At 1:11 a.m. the following night, a woodstove inside an unoccupied home on North Avenue produces a fire whose cause investigators can never confirm, because no one was present to supply the missing testimony.

4

Urbana, Illinois — Less than eleven hours later, a second vacant structure — this one already condemned by its own emptiness — catches at the rear wall, and a defensive attack is declared because there is no longer anything inside worth an offensive one.

5

The custody closes not with an arrest or a cause determination but with three case numbers filed in three counties, each one unaware it was ever party to the other two.

The heat was never anyone's to keep; it only ever needed somewhere empty to go.
