Working Theory — The Adjacency Clause

The Adjacency Clause holds that no record filed within the same rolling news cycle is truly unrelated to any other — that the same administrative hour which clears a warrant in Washington State is the hour that also voids a food label in Texas and reopens a trademark dispute in Virginia, because bureaucratic time moves as a single continuous surface rather than as separated jurisdictions. Under the Clause, the three exhibits are not coincidence but concurrent symptoms of one underlying audit sweeping quietly through the week.
